# 空气琴弦 · Air Strings

Draw strings in the air with your hands (or the mouse) and pluck them to play.
Pick an instrument (guitar, guzheng, pipa, harp, ukulele, electric guitar, music box) and a scale.

用双手（或鼠标）在空中拉出琴弦，再拨动它们弹奏。可选乐器和音阶，支持中英文。

Hand and face tracking run in your browser with MediaPipe. Camera video is never uploaded.
